MAKE YOUR WEBSITE PUBLIC

1. Extract this ZIP file.
2. Upload ALL extracted files and the assets folder to the main folder of a
   public HTTPS website. index.html must be at the top level of the website.
3. Open the web address your hosting service gives you and share that address.

These files do not require ChatGPT sign-in. Public access is controlled by
the web hosting service you choose; set that website to public.

No build step or package installation is needed for hosting these files.
Keep the filenames and folder structure unchanged. The assets folder,
JavaScript files, fonts, and WebAssembly file must all be uploaded.

Host at the root of your website address, such as https://your-domain.example/,
rather than inside a subfolder. HTTPS is required for the browsing features.
Opening index.html directly from your computer will not run the full app.

FEATURE AVAILABILITY

The download contains the SpadeX frontend. Browsing, games, AI, and music rely
on external providers and remain subject to their availability and access
rules. Their server infrastructure is not included in this package. Account
sign-in and payments are disabled until you connect your own services.
